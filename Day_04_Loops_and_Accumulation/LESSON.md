# Day 4: Loops and Accumulation


## Core idea

A loop repeats a rule. An accumulator remembers the result so far, such as a total or count.

## Learn

- `for`: repeat a known number of times.
- `foreach`: process every item in a collection.
- `while`: repeat while a condition remains true.
- `break`: stop the loop; `continue`: skip to the next iteration.
